Kisapmata

Eye screening and patient records for International Eye Center, Robinsons Starmills, City of San Fernando, Pampanga.

What it does

During an examination, the ophthalmologist photographs the front of the eye with a slit lamp. A screening model checks the photograph for 13 conditions, such as cataract, keratitis and pterygium, and the ophthalmologist reviews every result before it is recorded.

Patients see a result in their portal only after the ophthalmologist has reviewed it and released it to them.

Kisapmata is a screening aid, not a diagnosis. Diagnosis, prescriptions and treatment are the responsibility of the attending ophthalmologist.

Accounts

Accounts are issued by the clinic; you cannot create one on this site. When the clinic creates your account, you receive an email with a temporary password and a 6-digit code that unlocks it.

Emails from Kisapmata

Account, appointment and reminder emails come from kisapmata.admin@gmail.com. Kisapmata's permission on that Gmail account is limited to sending these emails. It cannot read the mailbox, and it does not access anyone else's Google account.

Your data

What Kisapmata collects, who can see it, and how to exercise your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 are set out in the privacy notice.

Kisapmata is operated by Pampanga State University for International Eye Center.
